## Why Wollastonite Is the Right Theme

Wollastonite is a calcium-silicate mineral, generally bright white, with distinctive needle-like or acicular fragments. It is used in ceramics, polymers, paints, metallurgy, construction products, and reinforcing applications.

This makes wollastonite an appropriate metaphor for your profile:

- **Structurally reinforcing:** You develop frameworks, systems, and technical solutions.
- **Versatile:** Your work spans chemical engineering, sustainability, analytics, product development, and entrepreneurship.
- **Materials focused:** Your strongest projects involve construction materials, waste valorisation, LCA, membranes, and industrial processes.
- **Quietly high-performing:** Wollastonite is functional rather than decorative, which fits a credible technical portfolio.
- **Networked:** Its acicular structure can become a visual metaphor for research, partnerships, finance, and implementation joining to create strength.

The website should not resemble a mining-company or geology website. The wollastonite influence should be subtle, technical, and sophisticated.

## Case-Study Writing Framework

Every case study should follow the same structure:

1. **Problem** — What challenge existed?
2. **Context** — Who or what was affected?
3. **Constraints** — What technical, financial, or operational limits applied?
4. **My role** — What did you personally own or contribute?
5. **Research** — What data, standards, or literature did you use?
6. **Analysis** — How did you evaluate alternatives?
7. **Solution** — What did you design or recommend?
8. **Impact** — What changed, expressed with defensible numbers?
9. **Reflection** — What did you learn and what would you improve?

Clearly distinguish personal work from the work of the wider team.

## 4. Working Method

Create a horizontal process section:

> **Research → Model → Evaluate → Validate → Scale**

### Research

Understand the material, market, users, standards, and operating environment.

### Model

Translate the problem into material flows, process models, decision frameworks, and commercial assumptions.

### Evaluate

Compare technical feasibility, environmental impact, economics, and implementation risk.

### Validate

Use data, stakeholder feedback, lifecycle tools, multi-criteria methods, and venture testing.

### Scale

Convert research into proposals, products, partnerships, and executable plans.

This section connects your chemical-engineering, sustainability, and entrepreneurial work into one coherent professional method.
